# Enable Tracking

The **Enable Tracking** setting controls the core functionality of Betterlytics on your WordPress site.

## How it works

When enabled, the Betterlytics tracking script is injected into the footer of your website. This allows Betterlytics to collect page views and other configured events.

*   **Enabled (Default)**: The tracking script is loaded, and data is collected.
*   **Disabled**: The tracking script is completely removed from your site source code. No data will be sent to Betterlytics.
